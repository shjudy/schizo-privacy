English

Summary: your audio never leaves your device. Sign-in is optional; the only data collected is account info (if you sign in) and usage statistics (only with your consent, and you can turn them off anytime — anonymous while signed out, linked to your account while signed in).

1. What we collect

  • Account info — only if you sign in (optional): social sign-in identifier (Apple, Google, or Kakao), email (Apple/Google; Kakao only with optional consent), display name. Every feature works without signing in.
  • Settings backup — only if you sign in (optional): app settings, theme, and preference values (JSON) for restore.
  • Usage statistics (only with your consent — anonymous while signed out, linked to your account while signed in): a randomly generated anonymous device ID, event names and timestamps, app/OS version. The values attached to an event are only non-identifying numbers, booleans, and in-app screen names — app launches and which tab was visited, which feature ran and whether it succeeded or failed, vertical-video export attempts and whether they finished or failed (file names, paths, and the video itself are never sent), whether a diagnosis or practice hint was shown on screen (never its text or your pitch numbers), whether the free-save limit notice or the full-version screen was reached and whether a purchase or restore succeeded (no amounts, transaction IDs, or account details), in-app story progress (whether a reveal was seen and which one, whether a reveal was replayed later from the Ward Journal and which one, how many ward journals were collected, whether the journal and puzzle screens were opened), a daily total of how many times the rotating chart cards appeared and how many were paused on and read, per deck (the deck label only — card text is never sent), and your consent itself. While you are signed out these statistics are anonymous; events sent while you are signed in carry your account identifier (unlinked when you delete your account in the app — section 3 below). Purpose: improving the product. Nothing is transmitted before you consent; anything recorded earlier stays on your device and is deleted immediately if you choose "Don't send". Turn it off anytime — the account screen in Studio mode, the "Stats" chip in Simple mode — and collection stops immediately, with anything still queued deleted.

2. What we never collect

  • Audio files, recordings, and processed results are never collected. All audio processing runs entirely on your device.
  • Microphone — Schizo (3.0 and later): used only when the Clinic’s “punch in” re-records a selected passage — Schizo’s single use of the microphone. (Voxscope’s and Tutti’s microphone use is covered in section 6.) Recordings are processed and stored on your device only and are never transmitted. You can revoke the permission anytime in System Settings › Privacy & Security › Microphone.
  • File names, song titles, and work content are never transmitted.
  • No ad identifiers, no tracking, no selling or sharing data for ads.

3. Retention and deletion

  • Account info and settings backups: deleted instantly via the in-app "Delete account" button; usage events are unlinked at the same time.
  • Usage statistics: kept up to 24 months, then deleted. Note: the automated deletion routine is still being put in place — collection began in July 2026, so no data has reached the 24-month mark yet, and the routine will be live before any does.
  • Voxscope's song requests and measurement history are covered separately in section 6.

4. Processors and data location

  • Supabase, Inc. — database and authentication hosting; data stored in the Sydney, Australia region (ap-southeast-2).
  • In-app purchases are handled by Apple's App Store (Apple's Privacy Policy).

5. Your rights

Use in-app account deletion, or contact: schizoapp@icloud.com

6. App-specific notes

Voxscope (iOS) — sections 1–5 apply as written, plus these two.

  • Song requests (optional): when you ask for a song to be added, only three values are sent — the song title, the artist name, and your app language setting (ko/en). No account or device identifier is sent with them, and no sign-in is required. It is a send-only inbox: the app cannot read requests back. Purpose: choosing which songs to add next. Send nothing and nothing is transmitted.
  • Microphone: used only to measure your voice during the intake. The recording is analysed on your device and the recording itself is never transmitted. You can revoke the permission anytime in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Microphone.
  • Measurement history sync — only if you sign in (optional): the numbers from your intake (range, comfortable range, accuracy, stability, timbre, dynamics, signal-to-noise ratio, vibrato) and the time of the measurement are stored linked to your account, so you get them back on a new device. Your voice recording itself is never transmitted — only the numbers left after analysis. If you do not sign in, your measurement history stays on your device only. It is deleted together with your account via in-app "Delete account".

Tutti (iOS) — shares this policy, but in practice nothing at all leaves your device.

  • There is no account and no sign-in, and no usage statistics are transmitted (the app has no transmission path at all). The account info, settings backup, and usage statistics in section 1 therefore do not apply to Tutti.
  • The microphone is used only to record your singing; recordings, harmonies, and exported files are processed and stored on your device only. You can revoke the permission anytime in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Microphone.
  • In-app purchases are handled by Apple's App Store; the developer only sees anonymized transaction data.
